Chocolate Varieties & Collection

Explore our complete collection featuring all 10 distinct types of handcrafted chocolates and ingredients.

1. 🍫 Dark Chocolate

Taste: Bitter or slightly sweet.
Ingredients: Cocoa solids, cocoa butter, and sugar.
Cocoa content: Usually 50–100%.
Best for: People who enjoy a rich chocolate flavor.

View Details

2. 🥛 Milk Chocolate

Taste: Sweet, creamy, and smooth.
Ingredients: Cocoa, milk powder or milk solids, cocoa butter, and sugar.
Best for: People who prefer a sweeter chocolate.

View Details

3. 🤍 White Chocolate

Taste: Very sweet, creamy, and buttery.
Ingredients: Cocoa butter, milk, and sugar.
Important: White chocolate contains no cocoa solids, which give dark and milk chocolate their brown color.

View Details

4. 🍫 Ruby Chocolate

Taste: Sweet with fruity and slightly tangy notes.
Color: Natural-looking pink or ruby.
Best for: Premium desserts and unique chocolate treats.

View Details

5. 🖤 Unsweetened Chocolate

Taste: Extremely bitter and intense.
Ingredients: Primarily cocoa solids and cocoa butter.
Best for: Baking brownies, cakes, and other desserts.

View Details

6. 🍫 Couverture Chocolate

Taste: Rich and smooth.
Special feature: Contains a relatively high proportion of cocoa butter, making it melt smoothly and develop a shiny finish when tempered correctly.
Best for: Professional chocolate making, truffles, and chocolate coatings.

View Details

7. 🍯 Caramel Chocolate

Taste: Sweet, buttery, and rich.
Special feature: Contains caramel or caramel-flavored filling.
Best for: People who love sweet, soft fillings.

View Details

8. 🌰 Nut Chocolate

Common nuts: Hazelnuts, almonds, peanuts, and pistachios.
Taste: Sweet, crunchy, and nutty.
Best for: A combination of chocolate and crunchy textures.

View Details

9. 🍫 Filled Chocolate

Features: Chocolate with a delicious filling inside.
Varieties: Caramel, strawberry cream, hazelnut cream, peanut butter, coconut, and coffee cream.
Best for: Indulgent treats with soft, flavorful centers.

View Details

10. 🍪 Chocolate with Add-ins

Features: Chocolate mixed with crunchy or savory ingredients.
Popular add-ins: Cookies, crispy rice, wafers, dried fruits, sea salt, and coffee beans.
Best for: Textured, exciting flavor combinations.

View Details
