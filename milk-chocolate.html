The Milk Chocolate Collection

1. Classic Milk Chocolate

The traditional baseline milk chocolate offering a sweet, comforting, and well-rounded cocoa flavor.

2. Creamy Milk Chocolate

Extra-rich dairy milk blended seamlessly into fine cocoa solids for an ultra-smooth, melt-in-your-mouth texture.

3. Swiss Milk Chocolate

Crafted following traditional Swiss conching techniques for an exceptionally velvety and refined palate finish.

4. Alpine Milk Chocolate

Made using premium mountain-pasture milk, giving it a characteristic robust and milky sweetness.

5. Extra-Creamy Milk Chocolate

Drenched in high-ratio milk solids designed specifically for those who love an intensely creamy dairy bite.

6. Milk Chocolate with Almonds

Crunchy roasted almond pieces studded generously throughout a smooth block of sweet milk chocolate.

7. Milk Chocolate with Hazelnuts

Whole roasted hazelnuts bundled inside thick, creamy milk chocolate to deliver a nutty, buttery crunch.

8. Milk Chocolate with Caramel

Flowing golden caramel ribbons encased in sweet milk chocolate for a decadent dual-flavor experience.

9. Milk Chocolate with Strawberry

Infused with real freeze-dried strawberry bits that add a fruity, slightly tart contrast to the sweet dairy cocoa.

10. Milk Chocolate with Cookies

Packed with crunchy chocolate cookie crumbs that balance the creamy sweetness with a satisfying bite.

11. Milk Chocolate with Crispy Rice

Light and airy puffed rice grains folded throughout the block for an effortless, airy crunch.

12. Milk Chocolate with Coconut

Flakes of toasted tropical coconut blended into rich milk chocolate for a fragrant, textured treat.

13. Milk Chocolate Truffles

Decadent whipped milk ganache centers coated in a smooth shell of fine dairy chocolate.

14. Milk Chocolate Buttons

Fun, bite-sized spherical milk chocolate buttons perfect for snacking, baking, or sharing.

15. Milk Chocolate Bites

Individually portioned squares of pure, creamy milk chocolate for a quick daily sweet indulgence.

16. Golden Butterscotch Milk Block

A masterclass in sweetness featuring crunchy buttery brittle fragments folded seamlessly into silky milk chocolate.
