The Nut Chocolate Collection

1. Chocolate with Hazelnuts

Whole roasted Italian hazelnuts embedded generously inside a thick block of rich, smooth chocolate.

2. Chocolate with Almonds

Crunchy California almonds roasted to a golden perfection and folded into decadent cocoa.

3. Chocolate with Peanuts

A classic combination of salted roasted peanuts paired against rich, creamy sweet chocolate.

4. Chocolate with Pistachios

Emerald green Mediterranean pistachio kernels studded throughout glossy, fine chocolate.

5. Chocolate with Cashews

Buttery, mild cashew nut pieces packed into a smooth and velvety chocolate foundation.

6. Chocolate with Walnuts

Earthv, textured walnut halves that complement the deep aromatic notes of artisan cocoa.

7. Chocolate with Pecans

Sweet, buttery pecan pieces folded into chocolate for a distinct southern-style crunch.

8. Chocolate with Macadamia Nuts

Exquisite, rich Hawaiian macadamia nuts delivering an ultra-buttery and luxurious bite.

9. Chocolate with Brazil Nuts

Large, nutrient-rich Amazonian Brazil nuts coated in a thick layer of fine artisanal chocolate.

10. Chocolate with Mixed Nuts

An ultimate medley of almonds, hazelnuts, cashews, and pecans blended into one rich bar.

11. Chocolate with Roasted Almonds

Intensely dry-roasted almonds offering an aromatic, smoky crunch with every mouthful.

12. Chocolate with Caramelized Hazelnuts

Hazelnuts glazed in golden caramel sugar shards before being enrobed in smooth chocolate.

13. Chocolate with Peanut Butter

A luscious, creamy core of whipped peanut butter encased inside a glossy chocolate shell.

14. Chocolate with Hazelnut Praline

Smooth, velvety hazelnut praline paste blended into a rich confectionary block.

15. Chocolate Nut Clusters

Hand-scoop clusters of assorted whole nuts bound tightly together with glossy melted chocolate.

16. Royal Toasted Nut and Praline Slab

A majestic artisanal masterpiece loaded with double-roasted hazelnuts, almonds, and creamy praline layers.
